WIKKEO · 3M+ MAU

A marketplace’s first paid product

The platform had no paid products at all. It had to start earning from sellers
without making them feel they had suddenly been put on the meter.

Role
Lead Designer
Timeline
2025 — 2026
Platforms
Web, Mobile
Results
80% of costs covered
Context

Plenty of traffic, none of it turning into revenue

WIKKEO is a marketplace with 3M+ monthly users. The platform ran with no revenue of its own: sellers listed for free, and the service earned nothing from any action inside the product. The brief was not “design a section” but “make the platform start earning from sellers without losing them”.

Prioritization

Out of many monetization options, two went into build

The first paid product sets how sellers treat every one after it. So I picked not by potential revenue but by three conditions at once: cheap to build, measurable effect, and value a seller can see.

  • ✓ A slot in the top sellers collection Cheap, fast, and the effect is transparent to the seller and the platform alike.
  • ✓ Paid notifications to buyers in chat Chat with a similar mechanic already worked — only monetization had to be added. Minimal engineering, with direct contact to a buyer who is already interested.
  • — Item promotion priced by views and purchases Slow and expensive to build, with no clear payback horizon.
  • — A subscription model At that point the platform had nothing to put inside subscription benefits — there was no access worth selling.
  • — Custom listing cards Unclear value and effect, expensive to build, and an easy way to clutter the interface with decoration.
  • — Extended customization of item descriptions No clear effect.
  • — Extra tools for describing an item The mechanic is not obvious to the seller and belongs to seller trust rather than to promotion.
  • — Push notifications Deferred until the mobile app ships.
  • — Sales boost Complex and expensive, and the real effect of a boost is hard to measure correctly. Deliberately deferred.
Solution

The seller sees what they are buying — before paying

The key requirement: the seller has to understand exactly what they get before the money leaves, not after. Both tools live in one Promotion section and are paid from a shared ad balance.

01

Ad balance

The seller tops up an internal account through moderation and then spends it on any promotion tool. Payment is separated from use — the payment barrier is crossed once, not before every action.

02

A slot in the top sellers collection

The seller buys a slot in the collection and gets extra traffic to their items. The first paid tool was chosen for exactly that: the effect is clear to both sides without explanation.

03

Targeted campaigns by segment

A campaign goes not to everyone but to people who already showed interest: viewed the item, ordered it, saved it or added it to the cart. The seller picks a ready-made offer and sees reach and final cost before confirming.

The buyer gets the offer where they are already talking

From the buyer’s side the new paid tool does not look like advertising: the offer arrives in the existing chat with the seller, in the same format they already use. That is what let monetization launch gently.

Results

The platform started earning
without forcing sellers

The platform got its first paid product — paid promotion in the seller dashboard, built from two tools on a shared ad balance. Monetization launched gently: the paid tools stay optional, and no mandatory listing fees appeared.
